Who We Are

Hi, we’re Group 4! This website is our way of capturing our younger selves our baby photos, favorite colors, life mottos, and the stories behind those precious snapshots. Life feels like it’s speeding by, and suddenly we’re college students already. We made this so we’ll always have a place to return to, to remember where we started and see how much we’ve grown. We also hope it helps you get to know the real us, who we were back then, and who we are today.

Project Goals

  • To preserve our precious childhood memories and stories in a simple, beautiful website that we can keep forever.

  • To create our own digital time capsule something we can come back to years from now and see exactly how far we've come.

  • To share who we truly are. our favorite colors, what we believe in, and the stories that shaped us.

  • To practice what we've learned in class by building a real website from scratch, one baby photo at a time.

  • To get to know each other better and grow not just as classmates, but as friends too.
